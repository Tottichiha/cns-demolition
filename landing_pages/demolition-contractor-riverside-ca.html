Riverside Demolition Permit & Utility Process

C&S Demolition handles the full permit process for every Riverside project. Here's what's involved:

  1. City of Riverside Building & Safety Permit — Demolition permit application submitted to the City of Riverside Building & Safety Division. Residential permits typically approved in 1–3 weeks.

  2. Riverside Public Utilities Coordination — Service disconnection for electricity and water through Riverside Public Utilities (RPU). SoCalGas disconnection coordinated separately.

  3. Environmental Clearance — SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires a certified asbestos and lead-containing materials survey for all structures before demolition permits are issued, regardless of construction date. C&S coordinates with licensed environmental consultants.

  4. SCAQMD Notification — South Coast Air Quality Management District notification required for qualifying structures 10 business days before demolition.

  5. Demolition & Site Cleanup — Work performed per permit. Final inspection attended by C&S to close the permit and confirm code compliance.
