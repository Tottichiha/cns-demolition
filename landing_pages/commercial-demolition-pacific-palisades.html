LADBS Commercial Demo Permit Process — Pacific Palisades

  1. Asbestos & Lead Survey (SCAQMD Rule 1403)Required for all commercial structures before permits are issued — regardless of construction date. Palisades Village commercial buildings have varied material profiles depending on construction era. C&S coordinates the survey with licensed environmental inspectors. If regulated asbestos is found, SCAQMD notification and abatement precede demolition.
  2. Utility DisconnectionGas (SoCalGas) and electric (LADWP or SCE depending on street) must be formally terminated. Written disconnection letters required as part of the LADBS application. Restaurant scopes require additional coordination for grease traps and hood systems with applicable inspection agencies.
  3. Fire Program Determination (if applicable)For January 2025 Palisades Fire-affected commercial properties, C&S identifies which City of LA or state-level expedited program applies to the parcel before filing the standard LADBS application. Expedited pathways may significantly reduce permit timeline.
  4. LADBS Demolition Permit ApplicationSubmitted with survey report, utility letters, and site plan identifying the structure and scope. C&S prepares and submits the complete application package.
  5. Post-Demo Inspection & Permit CloseoutLADBS requires inspection after demolition before the permit is closed. Required before new construction permits are issued on the same footprint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who issues commercial demolition permits in Pacific Palisades?

LADBS — the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety. Pacific Palisades is within the City of Los Angeles. For fire-affected commercial properties, City of LA expedited permit programs may apply. C&S identifies the correct permit pathway at the site visit.

How long does LADBS commercial permitting take?

Standard LADBS commercial demolition permits typically take 3–5 weeks once the application is complete. If asbestos is found and SCAQMD notification is required, add at minimum 10 business days before demo can begin. Expedited fire-rebuild programs can significantly compress this timeline for eligible properties. TI work covered under a remodel permit can start sooner once that permit is issued.

Can C&S work in Palisades Village without disrupting neighboring businesses?

Yes. Dust containment, neighbor protection, and debris removal protocols designed for active commercial environments are standard on Village multi-tenant projects. Work hours are coordinated with property management to minimize impact on adjacent businesses. C&S reviews the specific building conditions before scheduling any Village TI demo.

Is the asbestos survey required even for fire-damaged structures?

Yes — SCAQMD Rule 1403 applies to all structures regardless of damage status or construction date. For fire-damaged buildings, the survey is part of the hazmat assessment that precedes any demolition activity. Fire damage can compromise or disperse asbestos-containing materials, making the survey even more critical on burned structures.
