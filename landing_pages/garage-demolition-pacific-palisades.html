Garage Demo in Pacific Palisades — Two Very Different Situations

Garage demolition in Pacific Palisades right now falls into two distinct categories. The first is post-fire teardown — structures that were damaged or destroyed in the January 2025 Palisades Fire and are being cleared as part of the rebuild process. The second is standard redevelopment — detached garages coming down for ADU construction, estate redevelopment, or structure replacement on properties unaffected by the fire.

Both paths require LADBS permits and an SCAQMD Rule 1403 asbestos and lead survey. But the pre-demo protocols, waste classification, and permit pathways are different enough that they shouldn't be treated as the same job. C&S identifies which situation applies to your property at the site visit and advises on the correct sequence before any permit application is filed.

Standard vs. Post-Fire Garage Demo — Key Differences

Standard Garage Demo
For garages unaffected by the fire — standard redevelopment, ADU site prep, or structure replacement.
  • SCAQMD Rule 1403 survey (all structures, any age)
  • Utility disconnection letters (gas, electric)
  • LADBS demolition permit
  • Standard demolition with licensed C&D disposal
  • Slab retained or removed per project drawings
  • Permit close after final inspection
Post-Fire Garage Demo
For structures damaged or destroyed in the January 2025 Palisades Fire.
  • Hazmat assessment before any demo begins
  • Fire debris classified under separate waste regs
  • Potential state/city expedited permit programs
  • SCAQMD Rule 1403 survey still required
  • Burned debris transported under hazmat manifest
  • Phase 1 / Phase 2 program coordination if applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pacific Palisades garage demo require LADBS or City of LA permits?

Pacific Palisades is within the City of Los Angeles — LADBS (Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety) issues demolition permits. For post-fire properties, additional City of LA and potentially state-level expedited programs may apply. C&S identifies the correct permit pathway for your parcel at the site visit.

Is asbestos testing required even for a small detached garage?

Yes — SCAQMD Rule 1403 applies to all structures regardless of size or construction date. A 200-square-foot detached garage requires the same survey as a 2,000-square-foot main house. C&S coordinates the survey with licensed environmental inspectors as part of the standard pre-permit process.

How does C&S handle garage demo on a hillside Palisades lot?

Equipment is selected based on actual access conditions assessed at the site visit. Compact excavators, walk-behind breakers, and hand-staging are used where full-size equipment cannot access the site. Debris is broken into manageable loads and staged to the nearest truck access point. No equipment operates on grades that exceed safe operating limits for that equipment.

Can the slab stay if I'm building an ADU?

Yes — if your ADU architect specifies slab retention, C&S removes the structure above grade and leaves the slab per your project drawings. If the structural engineer determines the slab needs to come out, C&S handles full removal including footings. The scope follows the project plans.
