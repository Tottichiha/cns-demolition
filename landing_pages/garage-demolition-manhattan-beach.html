Manhattan Beach Garages Aren't Like Most Garages

If you've spent any time looking at homes in Manhattan Beach's Sand Section, you know the garage situation is unusual. The lots are narrow — some as tight as 25 feet wide — and parking is accessed from the alley behind the property rather than the street in most cases. Many Sand Section homes have tandem garages: two cars parked one behind the other in a single-width garage that runs the depth of the lot. Some have the garage on the ground floor with living space above.

When these structures need to come down, the alley is almost always the only practical equipment access point. And the alleys behind Sand Section homes are shared — meaning the equipment that needs to get in also has to accommodate whatever else is happening in that alley. Garbage trucks, deliveries, neighbors parking. C&S scouts the alley access conditions before scheduling, not after the excavator is already on the street.

Tree Section and Hill Section garages are typically more accessible — wider lots, more staging room, standard detached garage configurations. But even in the Tree Section, mature street trees along the named avenues create overhead clearance considerations for any equipment that needs to work near them.

Alley work in the Sand Section: If your garage backs to a narrow alley shared with neighbors, C&S will notify adjacent property owners before equipment arrives. Debris hauling from Sand Section alleys typically requires multiple smaller loads rather than one big haul — which is accounted for in the quote. We don't block an alley without warning the people who use it daily.

Garage Demolition Types in Manhattan Beach

Tandem Garage Removal

Common in the Sand Section. Single-width structure, two-car depth. Often integral to the main structure's lower floor. Removal may expose structural elements of the main home — structural engineer review required before scope is finalized. C&S does not proceed on integrated tandem garages without the structural drawings in hand.

Detached Garage Teardown

Freestanding garage structures in the Tree Section and Hill Section. More straightforward scope than integrated Sand Section garages. Permit required, asbestos survey required for pre-1979 structures. Access via side yard or alley confirmed before equipment scheduling.

ADU Site Prep

Existing garage demolished to make way for a permitted ADU. Manhattan Beach is an active ADU market. Demo permit and ADU construction permit are coordinated — C&S works with your architect and the City Building Division so the ADU permit is staged behind the demo completion, not delayed waiting for permit closure.

Garage Slab Removal

Concrete garage slab removed as part of garage demo or standalone for site preparation. Sand Section garage slabs are often poured against adjacent property's foundation — care required at the edges. C&S confirms boundary conditions and any shared foundation proximity at the site walk before slab demo begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my Manhattan Beach garage require an asbestos survey before demolition?

SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires asbestos and lead-containing materials surveys for all structures before demolition permits are issued — regardless of when the garage was built. The City of Manhattan Beach will not issue a demolition permit without the survey. Pre-1979 garages in the Sand Section frequently have positive findings in roofing materials, floor tile adhesive, and duct insulation. Post-1979 garages still require the survey, though positive findings are less common.

How do I know if my garage has a party wall with the neighbor?

In the Sand Section, some garages are built up to the property line and may share a wall — or be positioned close enough that demolition debris from one property could affect the other. C&S identifies this condition at the site walk. If a shared or close-proximity wall situation is confirmed, a structural engineer's input on the demolition approach is required before work begins. This is for your neighbor's protection as much as yours.

Can the garage demo be timed with the ADU construction permit in Manhattan Beach?

Yes — and timing this correctly matters. The goal is to have the ADU construction permit issued or in late-stage review before the demo begins, so the site is cleared and immediately available for construction. If the demo completes months before the ADU permit issues, you've created an open lot situation that may have additional requirements. C&S coordinates the demo timing with your architect's ADU permit submittal schedule to minimize the gap between demo completion and construction start.
