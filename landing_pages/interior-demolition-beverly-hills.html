Scope Breakdown — What Gets Removed and How

Walls & Partitions

Interior non-structural walls removed per demo plan. Load-bearing walls require engineer approval and temporary shoring before removal. C&S does not proceed with load-bearing demo without stamped structural drawings in hand.

Kitchen Strip-Out

Full removal of cabinetry (upper and lower), countertops, appliances, range hood, tile backsplash, and plumbing rough-in down to stub-outs. Salvage of specific items coordinated with owner if requested.

Bathroom Demo

Tile removal from walls and floors, fixture removal (toilet, vanity, tub, shower), cement board and waterproofing layer tear-out, and floor drain area preparation. C&S removes all materials to the framing.

Floor Systems

Tile removal with underlayment, hardwood floor removal, subfloor removal if needed, and concrete topping slab removal with jackhammer or saw-cut method depending on thickness and access.

Ceiling Removal

Drywall ceiling tear-down, plaster ceiling removal (common in older Beverly Hills homes), acoustic tile removal, and soffit demolition. Coffered or detailed ceilings require careful sequencing to protect preserved adjacent areas.

MEP Rough-In Removal

Exposed ductwork, plumbing lines within walls, and electrical conduit removed as part of gut scope when specified. This work is coordinated with your MEP subcontractors to ensure demo sequence aligns with the new install plan.

SCAQMD Rule 1403 — Asbestos Survey Required Before Interior Demo

SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires an asbestos and lead-containing materials survey for all structures before demolition work that disturbs building materials — regardless of the structure's age or construction date. Beverly Hills estates commonly contain asbestos in plaster walls, floor tile adhesive, ceiling texture, and duct wrap. C&S coordinates the survey before any work begins. If regulated asbestos is identified in the scope area, abatement is completed before demo proceeds in that zone.

Protecting a Beverly Hills Estate During Interior Work

Interior demolition in a high-value home requires a level of property protection that not every contractor prioritizes. Dust migration, debris vibration against preserved finishes, and equipment handling in tight spaces with existing flooring all require deliberate management.

Dust Containment

C&S installs plastic barrier systems at demo zone perimeters before any work begins. For larger gut projects, negative air pressure containment with HEPA filtration is used to prevent fine dust from migrating to occupied or preserved areas of the structure. Exterior HVAC intakes and openings adjacent to the demo area are sealed.

Preservation of Adjacent Areas

Hardwood floors, stone surfaces, and architectural millwork in areas adjacent to the demo zone are protected with hardboard, foam padding, or appropriate covering before any equipment enters those spaces. C&S documents conditions with photos before and after — standard practice on every Beverly Hills interior project.

Debris Removal

Interior demo debris is hand-carried or wheeled out — no dragging across preserved floors. Debris is staged in designated areas and removed in controlled loads. No open dumpsters on Beverly Hills residential streets longer than permitted — C&S schedules regular haul pickups to keep the property clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does interior demolition in Beverly Hills require a permit?

Cosmetic demo — finishes, flooring, non-structural elements — typically doesn't require a permit. Structural work including load-bearing wall removal, changes to MEP systems, or anything involving the building envelope requires a permit from the City of Beverly Hills Building & Safety. C&S advises on permit requirements based on your specific scope during the site consultation.

Does SCAQMD Rule 1403 apply to interior demo?

Yes. Rule 1403 applies to any demolition work that disturbs building materials, including interior demolition. An asbestos and lead survey is required before work begins — regardless of the structure's age. Older Beverly Hills homes have asbestos in plaster, floor tiles, duct insulation, and joint compound. C&S coordinates the survey and sequences abatement before demo in any affected areas.

Can C&S work from my architect's demo drawings?

Yes — and it's the preferred approach. Architect-prepared demo drawings allow C&S to scope the job precisely, sequence work correctly around structural elements, and avoid any ambiguity about what stays and what goes. If you don't have demo drawings yet, C&S can walk the property and document what we're scoping before submitting a quote.

How does debris removal work for interior demo?

Debris is hand-carried out of the structure to avoid dragging across preserved finishes. It's staged in a designated area and removed in scheduled loads. C&S doesn't leave large debris piles in driveways or on the street longer than necessary — Beverly Hills code enforcement and neighbor expectations make clean job site management non-negotiable.
