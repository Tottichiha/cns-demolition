What Is Selective Demolition?

Selective demolition — also called partial demolition — is the precise removal of specific structural or interior elements while leaving the rest of the building fully intact and operational. It's the right approach when a full teardown isn't needed or isn't practical.

Common selective demolition scenarios:

  • A commercial tenant improvements (TI) project that requires gutting a specific suite while adjacent spaces remain occupied
  • A restaurant remodel where the kitchen is completely rebuilt but the dining area is preserved
  • Removing a specific wall or structural element to open a floor plan
  • Phased renovation where one floor or wing is demolished while the rest of the building stays in use
  • Historic preservation projects requiring careful removal around protected facade or structural elements

Santa Ana has a large inventory of older commercial buildings — many along its active commercial corridors — where selective demolition is the right and only practical approach.

Our Selective Demolition Process

  1. Pre-Demo Scope Documentation — Before a single hammer swings, C&S documents exactly what is to be removed and what is to be preserved. Drawings, photos, and written scope are confirmed with you and your GC.

  2. Protection of Preserved Elements — Adjacent flooring, walls, MEP systems, and building facades are protected with appropriate barriers. We treat your building with care.

  3. Precise Removal — Our crews use a combination of manual and mechanical techniques — hand tools where precision is required, equipment where efficiency matters. No collateral damage.

  4. Debris Sorting & Removal — Demolished material is sorted on-site (recyclable vs. landfill) and hauled away promptly. We don't leave piles of debris sitting in your building.

  5. Clean Handoff — Site swept and ready for the next contractor — framer, MEP rough-in crew, tile contractor, or whoever's next in the schedule.
