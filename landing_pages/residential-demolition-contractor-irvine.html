Irvine Demolition Permit Process

The City of Irvine requires a demolition permit for any structural removal, including garage teardowns. Here's what to expect when you hire C&S Demolition:

  1. Free Site Assessment — We visit the property, assess the structure, identify any hazardous materials (asbestos, lead paint in pre-1978 homes), and prepare a detailed project scope.

  2. Permit Application Submitted — C&S prepares and submits the demolition permit application to the City of Irvine Building & Safety Division. We handle utility clearance forms for SoCalEdison and SoCalGas.

  3. Permit Approval (2–4 Weeks) — Irvine's building department typically reviews demolition permits in 2–4 weeks. C&S follows up proactively to avoid delays.

  4. Demolition Day(s) — Our crew arrives on schedule with all equipment. Most residential jobs are completed in 1–3 days. Site is cleaned and graded when we're done.

  5. Final Inspection — We schedule and attend the final city inspection to close the permit and confirm code compliance.
