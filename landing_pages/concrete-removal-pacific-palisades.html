Concrete Removal on Palisades Terrain — Why Access Drives Everything

Pacific Palisades concrete removal is fundamentally a terrain problem. The Palisades sits on a series of ridges, canyons, and terraced hillsides that create extreme variation in access conditions — even between adjacent properties. A driveway on a Huntington Palisades flat lot near Sunset is a straightforward excavator job. That same driveway square footage on a Castellammare parcel with a 30% grade and no turnaround is an entirely different scope requiring different equipment, different staging, and a different debris removal plan.

C&S assesses every Pacific Palisades concrete removal site in person before committing to a scope. The terrain conditions, driveway access, equipment clearance, and debris staging options are confirmed at the site visit — not estimated over the phone based on square footage alone.

Method Selection on Hillside Properties

Hydraulic Breaker (Excavator-Mounted)

Used where excavator access is available — flat sections of driveway, accessible patio areas, and pool decks reachable from level ground. Fastest method when site conditions allow.

Concrete Saw Cutting

Used for partial removal boundaries, scoring thick reinforced slabs before breaking, and cutting in areas where hydraulic impact would damage adjacent structures. Flat-saw and hand-saw depending on geometry.

Hand Jackhammers & Walk-Behind Breakers

Required on steep grades, tight canyon driveways, and terraced areas where excavator access is not possible. Slower, but the only viable approach in constrained Palisades hillside conditions. C&S crews are experienced with full hand-demo operations on difficult terrain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does concrete removal in Pacific Palisades require a permit?

Work in the public right-of-way (driveway apron near the street) requires a City of LA encroachment permit. Interior site concrete removal — pool decks, patios, slabs — generally does not require a standalone permit unless it is part of a larger permitted project. Post-fire site clearing may fall under separate expedited city programs. C&S advises during the site visit.

How does C&S remove concrete on a 25% grade driveway?

Concrete on steep grades is broken in place using hydraulic breakers or hand jackhammers depending on equipment access. Pieces are broken small enough to hand-stage or crane-stage down to a truck loading point at grade. Nothing is dragged down a slope — debris always moves in controlled, hand-carried or mechanically staged sequences. The specific staging plan is confirmed at the site visit.

Can retaining walls be removed without destabilizing the hillside?

Yes — when the sequencing is correct. That sequence requires a structural or geotechnical engineer to assess the wall's role in slope stability and specify how removal proceeds. C&S does not begin retaining wall demo without that assessment in hand. On Palisades hillside properties this is always the starting point for retaining wall removal.

How is concrete recycled after removal?

All concrete is hauled to licensed recycling facilities where it is crushed into aggregate for reuse. Rebar is cut free on-site and recycled separately as scrap metal. Disposal documentation provided on request.
