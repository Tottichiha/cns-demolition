LA Commercial Demolition Permits & Compliance

Commercial demolition in Los Angeles involves multiple agencies. C&S Demolition manages the entire compliance process so your project stays on schedule:

  1. LADBS Demolition Permit — Application submitted to the LA Department of Building and Safety. Includes site plan, structural assessment, and SCAQMD Rule 1403 asbestos/lead clearance documentation — required for all structures regardless of construction date.

  2. Utility Disconnection — Coordination with LADWP (electrical), SoCalGas, and LA DWP water to obtain service termination letters before demolition begins.

  3. SCAQMD Notification — For structures over 160 square feet or containing regulated asbestos, South Coast Air Quality Management District notification is required at least 10 business days before demo.

  4. Environmental Clearances — Asbestos and lead paint surveys for qualifying structures. C&S coordinates with licensed environmental consultants.

  5. Demolition & Site Cleanup — Work performed per approved permit. Final inspection with LADBS inspector to close the permit.
