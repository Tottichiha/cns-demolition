Types of Commercial Demo Work in Beverly Hills

Tenant Improvement (TI) Demo

Interior gut-out of a commercial space to prepare for new tenant or new use. Walls, ceilings, flooring, and MEP systems removed while the building shell stays intact. Most common commercial demo type in Beverly Hills's active retail and office market.

Full Commercial Teardown

Complete structure removal including shell, foundation, and site clearing. Required for redevelopment projects where the existing building no longer serves the site's value. Beverly Hills City permits required; asbestos survey mandatory for all structures per SCAQMD Rule 1403.

Medical / Dental Office

Medical office demolition involves specific handling considerations — lead-lined rooms, X-ray equipment, specialized plumbing, and HVAC systems require coordination with your environmental consultant. C&S works alongside the appropriate specialists for disposal of regulated materials.

Restaurant Demolition

Commercial kitchen removal, hood and exhaust system demolition, grease trap identification, tile and quarry floor removal, and full restaurant interior strip-out. Grease trap decommissioning is coordinated with a licensed plumbing contractor before demo begins.

SCAQMD Rule 1403 for Commercial Structures

SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires an asbestos and lead-containing materials survey for all commercial structures before any demolition permits are issued — regardless of when the building was built. Commercial buildings commonly contain asbestos in floor tile adhesive, ceiling tile, duct insulation, pipe wrap, and spray-applied fireproofing. C&S coordinates with licensed environmental consultants to complete the survey and manage abatement if regulated materials are found.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who issues commercial demolition permits in Beverly Hills?

The City of Beverly Hills Building & Safety Division issues commercial permits. The process involves asbestos/lead survey documentation (required for all structures per SCAQMD Rule 1403 regardless of age), utility disconnection letters, and a permit application with site plan. C&S manages the full permit process for commercial demolition projects.

What's the difference between TI demo and full commercial teardown?

TI demolition strips the interior of a commercial space — walls, ceilings, flooring, MEP systems — while the building envelope stays intact. Full commercial demolition brings the entire structure down. Both require permits; full teardowns require additional site clearing and foundation work. C&S handles both scopes.

Does the asbestos survey apply to newer commercial buildings too?

Yes. SCAQMD Rule 1403 applies to all structures regardless of construction date. Commercial buildings from any era may contain asbestos in various building materials. C&S coordinates the survey before any permit is submitted, which ensures no delays once the permit is in process.

Can C&S work around neighboring active tenants?

Yes. C&S plans commercial demo work around neighboring business hours, uses dust containment barriers, controls noise during restricted hours, and manages staging to avoid impacts on adjacent tenants. All scheduling and site management plans are discussed with the property manager before mobilization.
