Northwood Garages in 2025: The ADU Opportunity and the Demo Reality

California's ADU laws have made attached garage conversion the most-discussed home improvement topic in Northwood. Converting a 400–500 square foot attached two-car garage into a rental unit adds significant value to a 1980s Northwood home — and it often begins with partial or complete garage demolition to remove the slab, existing framing, and systems before a builder reconstructs to residential code.

What most Northwood homeowners do not realize until they are mid-project is that the approval sequence is inverted from what feels logical. The instinct is to pull a City of Irvine permit first, then notify the HOA. In practice, the Northwood Community Association's Architectural Review Committee must approve any structural change before the city will issue a permit. Miss that sequence and your project stalls at the permit counter with incomplete submittal documents.

HOA Sequencing Warning: Northwood Community Association ARC reviews are scheduled on a fixed calendar. If you miss the submission deadline for a given cycle — typically two to three weeks before the board meeting — you wait another month for the next review. C&S Demolition prepares ARC submittal packages as part of our pre-project coordination so you hit the next available meeting, not the one after that.

Why 1980s Northwood Garages Are Higher Risk Than They Look

The original Northwood housing stock was largely completed by 1992. In that era, several building materials used in attached garages are now known asbestos-containing building materials (ACBM). The drywall joint compound (mud) used on garage walls and ceilings was commonly asbestos-reinforced through the late 1970s and into the early 1980s. Vinyl floor tile and, more importantly, the black cutback adhesive beneath it, was a standard asbestos carrier through approximately 1984. If your garage has a vinyl tile floor with dark adhesive beneath, there is a meaningful probability it contains asbestos.

Additionally, if the garage shares an HVAC system with the main house — common in Northwood floor plans — the duct wrap insulation and any duct tape used in that era may be suspect. This is not a reason to delay your project; it is a reason to survey before demolition, which SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires regardless of age.

SCAQMD Rule 1403: All structures — including detached and attached garages — require a certified asbestos survey before the City of Irvine will issue a demolition permit. There is no exemption for post-1980 construction. C&S Demolition coordinates survey scheduling so lab results do not delay your permit application.

Detached vs. Attached: The Demo Scope Is Different

Northwood has both. The original planned-community layouts along streets like Bryan Ave and Yale Ave include many attached two-car garages that share a wall and roofline with the main house. Detached garages appear more often in corner lots and certain tract configurations. The demolition scope differs significantly. An attached garage removal requires careful structural analysis of the shared wall, temporary shoring in some cases, and a weather-tight finish on the exposed wall before permit closeout. A detached garage is a simpler envelope — the SCAQMD survey still applies, but structural complexity is reduced.

The Approval Sequence for a Northwood Garage Demo

1

Confirm Your HOA Section

Original Northwood (Northwood Community Association) or Northwood Pointe — the ARC form, submittal address, and meeting calendar are different for each. We confirm your section on the first site visit.

2

ARC Package Preparation

We prepare the architectural review submittal including scope description, site plan, and any required drawings. For garage-to-ADU conversions, the ARC package must reflect the new residential use, not just the demolition phase.

3

SCAQMD Asbestos Survey

Certified industrial hygienist samples suspect materials. For 1980s Northwood attached garages, this typically includes drywall compound, floor tile and adhesive, and any HVAC duct materials in the garage space. Lab results in 5–10 business days.

4

City of Irvine Demolition Permit

With HOA approval and SCAQMD survey in hand, we submit to City of Irvine Building & Safety. Northwood's established neighborhood infrastructure means straightforward permit reviews for most single-structure demolition projects.

5

Utility Disconnects & Demo

SCE subpanel disconnect, SoCalGas service to garage (if applicable), and IRWD water to any utility sink. Equipment access is straightforward on Northwood's flat grid — Bryan, Yale, Culver, Irvine Blvd all allow full debris truck routing.

6

Final Inspection & Site Cleanup

City of Irvine final inspection, site graded and cleaned. If you are proceeding to ADU construction, we coordinate the handoff to your contractor with a clean, permit-closed site.

Northwood's Flat Terrain Helps — Shared Driveways Need a Conversation First

Most Northwood lots have direct street access with adequate turnaround for a standard excavator and debris truck. The flat terrain — unlike hillside Irvine villages — means equipment mobilization is efficient and predictable. However, some Northwood tract configurations include shared or easement driveways where equipment staging affects a neighboring property. In those cases, we address access logistics before scheduling the demo crew, not on the morning of demo day.

If your garage or driveway configuration involves a shared approach, we document the access plan and, where appropriate, communicate with the adjacent property owner in advance. This is standard practice in HOA communities, and the Northwood Community Association's rules reinforce it.
