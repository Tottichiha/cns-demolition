Interior Demo in 1970s Mission Viejo — Read This Before You Start

If you're planning a remodel in one of Mission Viejo's original neighborhoods — Aegean Hills, the original Casta del Sol tracts, the homes along Mustang Run and Pebblecreek — there's something worth knowing before you schedule any interior demolition: popcorn ceiling texture applied before 1979 commonly contains asbestos. And in Mission Viejo, a lot of ceilings were textured before 1979.

This matters because you can't just scrape popcorn ceiling in a pre-1979 home and haul it out as regular demo debris. SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires the material to be sampled before it's disturbed. If it tests positive for regulated asbestos, it has to be removed by a licensed abatement contractor — not a demo crew — and SCAQMD must be notified at least 10 business days before abatement begins.

About 70% of Mission Viejo homes built before 1978 have asbestos in their popcorn ceilings.

That's not an exaggeration — it's what surveys in these neighborhoods consistently show. C&S doesn't skip the environmental assessment to get the job started faster. We identify the condition early, coordinate the testing, and give you a real scope of work with realistic timing before any interior demo begins. A quick interior demo that triggers a stop-work for undisclosed asbestos isn't quick at all.

For homes built after 1980 — in Painted Trails, Oso Viejo, the Trabuco Canyon Road neighborhoods — interior demo is more straightforward. No mandatory asbestos survey, no SCAQMD notification requirements. Those projects move faster and the risk profile is different. C&S prices and schedules each project based on the actual conditions, not a blanket approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

My Mission Viejo home was remodeled in 1995 — does the popcorn ceiling still have asbestos?

Depends on whether the popcorn ceiling was part of that 1995 remodel or carried over from original construction. If the ceiling was re-textured in 1995 with materials from that era, asbestos is not a concern. If the 1995 remodel left the original 1972 ceiling in place and just painted over it, the original texture may still be present and still require sampling. A visual inspection can't distinguish original from replacement texture — that's what sampling is for. C&S identifies ceiling construction history at the site walk before recommending a testing approach.

Can I do the popcorn ceiling test myself to save time?

SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires the asbestos survey to be conducted by a certified asbestos consultant (CAC). DIY sampling kits don't satisfy the regulatory requirement — the sample collection method and chain of custody have to meet SCAQMD standards. C&S coordinates with licensed consultants who perform SCAQMD-compliant surveys. Trying to shortcut the testing phase typically creates more delay, not less.

How long does interior demo take in a typical Mission Viejo home?

The physical demo work — kitchen gut, bathroom tearout, or a few rooms — typically runs 1–3 days. If asbestos abatement is required first, add the abatement timeline plus the mandatory SCAQMD 10-business-day notification period. Total time from project initiation to completed demo for a pre-1979 Mission Viejo home with confirmed asbestos is typically 4–7 weeks. Post-1979 homes without regulated materials can often be done in 2–3 weeks from first contact.

Does interior demolition in Mission Viejo require a permit?

Cosmetic interior demolition — removing tile, cabinets, flooring, and non-structural drywall that will be replaced in kind — typically does not require a standalone demolition permit in Mission Viejo. Structural wall removal, changes to MEP systems, or scope that's part of a larger remodel typically requires permits pulled under the remodel or renovation permit. C&S confirms the permit requirement for your specific scope with the City of Mission Viejo Building & Safety Division before scheduling work.
