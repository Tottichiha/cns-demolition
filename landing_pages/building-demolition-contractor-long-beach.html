Long Beach Demolition Permits & Environmental Compliance

Demolition in Long Beach involves multiple agencies and compliance requirements. C&S manages the entire process:

  1. City of Long Beach Development Services Permit — Demolition permit application submitted to the City of Long Beach. Commercial and industrial projects may require additional review and environmental clearance documentation.

  2. Asbestos & Lead Paint Survey — Required for all pre-1980 structures. C&S coordinates with licensed environmental consultants to complete surveys and arrange abatement prior to demolition commencement.

  3. SCAQMD Notification — The South Coast Air Quality Management District requires written notification at least 10 business days before demolition begins for qualifying structures. C&S prepares and submits all SCAQMD notification paperwork.

  4. Utility Disconnections — Coordination with Southern California Edison, SoCalGas, and Long Beach Water Department to obtain service termination confirmation before demo begins.

  5. Demolition, Haul-Out & Final Inspection — Work completed per permit scope. Final inspection with City of Long Beach inspector to close all open permits.
