Before You Demo a Santa Monica Garage — Check the Parking Math

Santa Monica's zoning code takes parking seriously in ways that affect garage demolition decisions more than in most California cities. If a detached garage provides required off-street parking for the main residence — and in Santa Monica's dense neighborhoods, it often does — removing it may require a parking replacement plan as part of the demolition and redevelopment permits.

State ADU law has created some carve-outs for this. Under current state law, a detached garage can generally be converted to or replaced by an ADU without a parking replacement requirement. But "replaced by an ADU" and "demolished as a standalone project without replacing the parking" are different scenarios. If you're removing a garage without immediately replacing it with an ADU, check with the City of Santa Monica Planning Division before scheduling any demolition work.

The short version: If you're converting an existing garage to an ADU — or demolishing a garage and immediately building an ADU on the same footprint — state ADU law largely covers the parking replacement question. If you're simply removing a garage without a replacement structure, confirm with Santa Monica Planning that the parking removal is permissible for your specific parcel before engaging C&S for the demolition work.

The physical demolition of Santa Monica garages follows the standard process: SCAQMD survey for all structures (positive findings are common in pre-1979 garages, and Santa Monica has a lot of pre-1979 garages), utility disconnection, City demolition permit, and demo. The permit process at Santa Monica Building & Safety typically takes 4–6 weeks for a complete application. Post-1979 garages still require the SCAQMD survey under current rules — there is no construction-date exemption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does state ADU law override Santa Monica's parking replacement requirements?

Largely yes, when an ADU is being built. Under Government Code Section 65852.2, local agencies cannot require parking replacement when a garage is converted to or demolished and replaced by an ADU. However, the specific application of this depends on the project scope and parcel characteristics. Santa Monica's Planning Division is the authoritative source on how this applies to your specific property — C&S handles the demolition permit, not the ADU entitlement or parking analysis.

Is the SCAQMD survey really required for a garage built in 1995?

Under current SCAQMD Rule 1403, a certified asbestos and lead-containing materials survey is required for all structures before demolition permits are issued — there is no construction-date exemption in the current rule text. This changed from how the rule was historically applied. C&S follows the current SCAQMD requirements, not the pre-2024 practice. For a 1995 garage, the survey is very unlikely to find regulated asbestos, but the survey must be conducted and submitted before the City issues the demolition permit.

My Santa Monica garage has a studio apartment above it. Does that affect the demolition process?

Significantly. If the studio above the garage is a rental unit, it may be subject to Santa Monica rent control. In that case, the Rent Control Board's demolition permit process applies — tenant relocation assistance, proper notice, and a hearing if the tenant contests. This is entirely separate from the building demolition permit. Engage a land use attorney before any demolition planning on this type of property.
