Anaheim Demolition Permits & Regulations

Every residential demolition in Anaheim requires a permit from the City of Anaheim Building Division. Here's what's involved:

  • Demolition Permit Application — Includes site address, structure description, and contractor licensing information. C&S handles the full application.
  • Utility Clearances — Letters of disconnection from SCE (electrical), SoCalGas, and the water authority must be obtained before the permit is issued.
  • SCAQMD Notification — For structures over 160 sq ft, the South Coast Air Quality Management District requires notification at least 10 business days before demolition begins.
  • Environmental Review — Pre-1978 homes require an asbestos/lead survey. Any regulated materials must be abated by a licensed contractor before demolition.
  • Grading Permit — If the lot requires significant re-grading after demolition, a separate grading permit may be required from the City of Anaheim.

C&S Demolition manages every step of this process. You don't need to navigate city hall — we do it for you.
