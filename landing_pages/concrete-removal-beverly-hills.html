Method Selection — How C&S Approaches the Work

Concrete removal isn't one method applied to every job. The approach depends on slab thickness, rebar content, site access, and what's adjacent to the removal zone.

Hydraulic Breaker (Excavator-Mounted)

The fastest method for large-area concrete removal on accessible flat properties. A hydraulic breaker head mounted on a compact or mid-size excavator breaks concrete quickly into manageable pieces that are then bucket-loaded into dump trucks. Used for driveways, large patio slabs, and pool decks where the machine can access the full area.

Concrete Saw Cutting

Used to create clean cut lines before breaking — particularly for partial removal where a precise boundary is needed. Also used to score thick slabs before hydraulic breaking, or to remove concrete in tight areas where a full excavator swing isn't possible. C&S uses flat-saw and hand-saw methods depending on the geometry.

Hand Demolition (Jackhammers)

Required when excavator access is not available — under a deck structure, in a tight courtyard, along a pool edge, or in narrow hillside areas. Slower but allows controlled removal in areas where equipment can't reach. C&S uses electric and pneumatic jackhammers depending on power availability at the site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does concrete removal in Beverly Hills require a permit?

It depends on the scope. Work near the public street (driveway apron removal) typically requires a Beverly Hills encroachment permit from Public Works. Interior site work — pool decks, patios, slabs — generally does not require a permit unless it's part of a larger permitted project. C&S advises on permit requirements based on your specific scope.

How do you handle concrete removal on tight hillside lots?

Hillside sites that limit equipment access are handled with a combination of smaller excavators, concrete saws, and hand jackhammer work. C&S sequences the work so debris is broken into loadable pieces and staged before any machine access is needed. We assess every hillside concrete project individually during the site visit — no standard plan works for every Trousdale or canyon property.

Can C&S do partial concrete removal with clean edges?

Yes. Saw-cutting creates a clean, straight joint at the removal boundary. This is standard practice for partial driveway replacement, partial patio removal, or any situation where new concrete will be poured adjacent to the removal area. Clean saw cuts prevent premature cracking in the remaining slab.

What happens to the concrete debris?

Concrete is broken, loaded, and hauled to licensed recycling facilities where it is crushed into aggregate for reuse. Rebar is cut out and recycled separately as scrap metal. C&S provides disposal documentation on request.
