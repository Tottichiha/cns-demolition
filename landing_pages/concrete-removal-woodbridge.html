The Woodbridge Concrete Situation

Driveways, patios, walkways, and pool decks throughout Woodbridge were poured when the village was developed — the majority between 1977 and 1984. That is four-plus decades of thermal cycling, tree root intrusion from the mature landscaping that now characterizes the community, and the normal heaving that comes from Southern California's expansive clay soils. The slabs that property owners want removed are thick, reinforced, and in many cases have cracked and settled in patterns that complicate clean removal.

Concrete removal in Woodbridge is also subject to community appearance standards administered by Woodbridge Village Association's Architectural Review Committee. Any exterior hardscape change — removing a driveway approach, demolishing a patio, eliminating a walkway — can require an ARC submittal showing what the property will look like after removal and what replaces the concrete surface. This is not bureaucratic friction unique to demolition; it reflects the HOA's mandate to maintain the community aesthetic that keeps Woodbridge property values strong.

North Lake and South Lake Lots — Additional Considerations

Properties on the lake-facing perimeter of Woodbridge's two man-made recreational lakes sit within sightlines that the HOA actively monitors. Concrete removal on these lots — particularly patio demolition that changes the visible footprint of a property from the lake path — may receive additional ARC scrutiny regarding what fills the space post-demo.

Erosion and runoff from these lots also intersects with IRWD jurisdiction. If your concrete removal exposes bare soil near a drainage easement or lake-adjacent grade, IRWD may require a temporary erosion control plan before the permit closes.

What We Remove

Driveway Slabs

Original Woodbridge driveways are typically 4–6 inch unreinforced or lightly rebar'd slabs. Many have settled at the apron edge where they meet the street approach. We break, remove, and haul — including the subbase if your contractor needs a fresh grade.

Patio Concrete

Rear patios in Woodbridge range from simple broom-finish slabs to multi-level concrete with planters. We remove all of it cleanly without disturbing adjacent landscaping or fencing — a common concern in the densely landscaped lots near the lakes.

Pool Decks

Woodbridge's older pool decks are frequently cantilever or broom-finish concrete with expansion joints and coping. We remove pool deck without undermining the pool shell, and coordinate with your pool contractor on how close to get to the bond beam.

Walkways & Approaches

Side-yard walkways, entry approaches, and garage aprons — all standard scope. Where the walkway ties into the public sidewalk, we follow City of Irvine requirements for how the approach termination is left.

Planters & Retaining Walls

Original concrete block planters and low retaining walls from the 1970s and 1980s are common in Woodbridge. We remove masonry and concrete block, including footings, and clean the area to whatever grade your landscaper or hardscape contractor needs.

Full Slab Removal

If you are clearing a lot or prepping for a new foundation, we remove the entire existing slab including footings. We work to your engineer's finish grade spec and document removal for the structural permit record.

Underground Utility Awareness in Woodbridge

Woodbridge was developed with underground utilities from the start — electrical, cable, and telephone are all sub-grade throughout the village. Irrigation lines for the HOA's extensive common-area landscaping also run through private lots in some sections near Jeffrey Rd and Culver Dr. Before any saw-cutting or breaking, we confirm utility locate clearance through USA/811 and visually check for IRWD irrigation tie-ins that are not always in the 811 database.

Recycling and Haul-Off

Concrete from Woodbridge removals is hauled to a certified concrete recycling facility. Clean concrete without excessive contamination is accepted for aggregate recycling. We separate rebar for metal recycling. Landfill disposal is used only when contamination levels require it, and we document disposal for any permitting records that require waste management certification.
