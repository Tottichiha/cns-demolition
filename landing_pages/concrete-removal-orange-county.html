How Concrete Removal Works

  • 1

    Free On-Site Estimate — We assess the slab thickness, rebar content, site access, and haul-out route. You get a written quote before any work begins.

  • 2

    Saw Cutting — Large slabs are saw-cut into manageable sections. This produces cleaner breaks and reduces vibration impact on adjacent structures.

  • 3

    Breaking & Loading — Jackhammers, hydraulic breakers, and bobcats break the concrete into rubble and load it directly into our dump trucks on-site.

  • 4

    Haul-Out — All concrete is transported from your Orange County property to certified concrete recycling facilities. Nothing is left behind.

  • 5

    Site Ready for Next Step — Once concrete is out, the area is cleaned up and ready for your landscaper, concrete installer, or paving contractor.

All concrete removed by C&S Demolition is delivered to certified recycling facilities. Crushed concrete is reused as road base, fill material, and aggregate — keeping it out of landfills. This is the right way to do it, and it's how we operate on every OC job.

What Affects Concrete Removal Cost in Orange County

Several factors influence the final price for concrete removal in OC:

  • Thickness — Standard residential slabs are 4". Driveways are often 4–6". Heavier commercial slabs can be 6–8" or more, requiring more breaking effort and generating more debris weight.
  • Rebar Density — Heavily reinforced concrete (common in foundations and commercial slabs) takes longer to break and generates more steel to separate and dispose of separately.
  • Site Access — Can our equipment reach the slab directly? Tight side yards, overhead obstructions, or hillside locations increase labor time and cost.
  • Disposal Distance — Longer haul distances to the recycling facility add a small cost. Most OC jobs are within standard range.
  • Square Footage — Larger slabs cost more but often have better per-square-foot pricing due to equipment efficiency.
