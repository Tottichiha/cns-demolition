California Demolition FAQ

Utility Disconnection Before Demolition in California

Which utilities to call, what clearances are required, and how to avoid the scheduling delays that hold up demolition permits.

A demolition permit in California will not be issued until the city has written confirmation that all utilities have been properly disconnected and capped. This is one of the most common causes of permit delays — not because the requirement is complex, but because property owners wait until after submitting the permit application to call the utilities, then discover that field crew scheduling is weeks out. Call early.

Why Utility Disconnection Is Required

Live utilities in a structure being demolished create serious safety hazards: gas leaks during demolition can cause explosions, live electrical lines can cause fires or electrocution, and active water lines flood newly opened excavations. California Code of Regulations Title 8 §1735 mandates that all utilities be shut off and capped before demolition begins. Cities enforce this by requiring written utility clearances as part of the permit package.

Key timing insight: Contact all utilities the same week you engage your demolition contractor — not after the permit is submitted, and not after the permit is issued. Most utility providers take 1–3 weeks to schedule a field crew for the disconnect. Starting early means the clearances arrive around the same time the city completes plan check, keeping your project on schedule.

Utility-by-Utility Guide for Southern California

🔥
Natural Gas — SoCalGas

Call SoCalGas to request a permanent service termination for demolition. They will schedule a field technician to physically shut off and cap the gas line at the meter or at the street. You will receive a written confirmation of termination — this is the clearance your permit package needs.

What to tell them: "I need a permanent demolition disconnect for [address] — a demolition permit is pending."

  • Do not simply stop paying the bill — a service termination is different from non-payment shutoff
  • Ensure the cap is performed at the city main or at a location that will not be disturbed by demolition
  • Scheduling lead time: typically 1–2 weeks for a field crew appointment
SoCalGas: 1-800-427-2200 | socalgas.com
⚡
Electricity — SCE or LADWP

Contact your electric utility to pull the meter and disconnect service at the structure. The utility will send a technician to remove the meter and physically cut the service drop from the pole or pad transformer.

  • SCE service area (most of Southern California outside LA proper): Call SCE's construction services line and request a "permanent service disconnection for demolition."
  • LADWP service area (City of Los Angeles): Request a demolition disconnect through LADWP's service order line.
  • Written confirmation of disconnect is required for the permit package
  • Scheduling lead time: 1–3 weeks
SCE: 1-800-655-4555 | sce.com | LADWP: 1-800-342-5397 | ladwp.com
💧
Water and Sewer — City Utility Department

Water disconnection is handled by your city's municipal water department (or a utility district in some areas). Request a permanent demolition disconnection — a simple meter turn-off is not sufficient; the service line must be capped at or near the main.

  • Contact your city's Public Works or Utilities department
  • Request "permanent water service termination for demolition"
  • A cap-off at the main is typically required to prevent back-siphonage during demolition
  • Water utilities are often the fastest to schedule — sometimes 3–7 business days
Contact your city's water/public works department — phone number varies by city.
📡
Telecom and Cable — Spectrum, AT&T, etc.

While telecom lines are not always required by the city for permit purposes, they should be removed before demolition to prevent damage to neighboring properties and to the provider's infrastructure on your line. Call each provider (cable, phone, fiber) and request service disconnection and line removal for demolition. Most providers remove their drop lines free of charge.

Contact your specific providers — Spectrum: 1-833-267-6094 | AT&T: 1-800-288-2020

Utility Disconnection Checklist

  • Called SoCalGas (or local gas provider) — demolition disconnect scheduled
  • Called SCE / LADWP — meter pull and permanent disconnect scheduled
  • Called city water department — permanent cap-off at main requested
  • Contacted cable/telecom providers for line removal
  • Received written confirmation from gas utility
  • Received written confirmation from electric utility
  • Received written confirmation from water department
  • All clearances included in permit application package
