Beverly Hills Garage Demo Permit Process

Beverly Hills is its own city with its own Building & Safety Division — not LA County. Here's what the permit process requires for a detached garage demolition:

  • Asbestos & lead survey (SCAQMD Rule 1403): Required for all structures before permits are issued, regardless of the structure's construction date. C&S coordinates with a licensed environmental inspector to complete the survey and assemble the report for the permit application.
  • Utility disconnection letters: Gas service (SoCalGas) and electrical service (SCE or LADWP depending on street location) to the garage must be formally terminated. Written confirmation from each utility is required by the City.
  • Demolition permit application: Submitted to the City of Beverly Hills Building & Safety with the survey report, utility letters, and site plan identifying the structure being removed. C&S prepares and submits the full application package.
  • SCAQMD notification (if asbestos found): If the survey identifies regulated asbestos, a 10-business-day notification to the South Coast AQMD is required before demolition begins. Abatement is completed first, then demolition proceeds.
  • Final inspection: Beverly Hills requires an inspector walkthrough after demolition to close the permit. This is needed before new structure permits can be issued for the same footprint.

Flat Lot vs. Hillside Garages — Not the Same Job

The approach to a 2-car detached garage on a flat Beverly Hills flats lot and a built-in garage on a Trousdale Estates hillside property are completely different in terms of equipment selection, staging, and safety requirements.

Flat-Lot Garages (Beverly Hills Flats)

Standard detached garages behind flat-lot estates are the most straightforward garage demo in Beverly Hills. Good street access, room for a compact excavator to maneuver, and standard staging for debris removal. Most flat-lot garages are demolished in a single day once the permit is in hand.

Hillside Garages (Trousdale, Benedict Canyon, the Hills)

Built-in and hillside garages present a different set of challenges. They often have retaining walls on multiple sides, steep driveways that restrict truck access, and neighbor structures within 10–20 feet. C&S uses compact reach excavators on hillside sites and plans debris removal around the access limitations of the specific driveway and road. We document pre-demo conditions on all hillside projects — photos of adjacent structures before any equipment arrives.

Carriage Houses and Multi-Car Estate Structures

Older Beverly Hills estates sometimes have carriage houses or multi-car structures that function somewhere between a garage and a secondary building. These are treated the same as any other detached structure for permit purposes — asbestos survey, utility disconnections, and City permit required. C&S manages the full scope regardless of how the structure is categorized.

Questions About Beverly Hills Garage Demolition

Does Beverly Hills require a permit for garage demolition?

Yes. The City of Beverly Hills Building & Safety Division requires a demolition permit for all detached structures. The application requires an asbestos/lead survey report (SCAQMD Rule 1403 — applies to all structures regardless of age), utility disconnection letters, and a site plan. C&S manages the entire permit process.

Can the slab stay for an ADU project?

Yes. If your ADU engineer or architect specifies slab retention, C&S removes the structure above grade and leaves the slab intact. If complete removal including slab and footings is required, C&S handles that too. The scope follows your project drawings.

What does the asbestos survey cover?

SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires an asbestos and lead-containing materials survey for every structure before demolition permits are issued — regardless of when the structure was built. C&S coordinates with a licensed environmental inspector. Survey results must accompany the permit application. If regulated asbestos is found, SCAQMD notification and abatement must happen before demolition.

What happens to the debris?

All debris is loaded on-site and hauled to licensed C&D recycling and disposal facilities. No dumpsters are left on-site longer than necessary — Beverly Hills residential streets have restrictions on extended dumpster placement. C&S provides disposal manifests on request.
