Orange County Demolition FAQs

Do I need a permit for demolition in Orange County?

Yes. All structural demolition in Orange County cities requires a demolition permit from the local building department. C&S Demolition handles the entire permit application process on your behalf, including utility clearances and any required inspections.

How much does demolition cost in Orange County?

Costs vary widely by project type. Residential house demolition typically ranges from $8,000 to $35,000. Interior demo for a kitchen or bathroom runs $1,500–$8,000. Pool removal ranges from $12,000 to $30,000. Concrete driveway removal starts around $500. Call us for a free on-site estimate.

How long does a residential demolition take in OC?

A typical single-family home demolition takes 1–3 days of active work. Permitting typically takes 2–4 weeks before demo can begin. C&S Demolition streamlines the permit process to minimize total project timeline.

Are you a licensed contractor in California?

Yes. C&S Demolition is fully licensed by the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB). We are also bonded and carry full general liability and workers' compensation insurance.

Do you handle debris removal after demolition?

Absolutely. All C&S Demolition projects include complete debris hauling and disposal. We sort materials on-site and deliver recyclables to certified facilities. You get a clean, graded lot at project completion.
