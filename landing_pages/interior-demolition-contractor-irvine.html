Structural vs. Non-Structural Walls in Irvine Homes

One of the most common questions we hear: "Can I remove this wall?" Here's what every Irvine homeowner needs to know:

Non-Structural (Partition) Walls

These walls carry no load from the structure above. They can typically be removed without a permit in simple cases, though we always recommend verifying with a building professional. C&S can identify likely non-structural walls during our free site assessment.

Load-Bearing (Structural) Walls

These walls support the weight of the floor or roof above. Removing them requires a permit from the City of Irvine, and a structural engineer must design a header or beam replacement. C&S coordinates with structural engineers and manages the permit process. Attempting to remove a load-bearing wall without proper engineering is dangerous and illegal.

When in doubt, call us before you start. A 15-minute site visit can save you from a costly mistake.
