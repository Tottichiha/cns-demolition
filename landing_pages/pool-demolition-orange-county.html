Pool Demolition Cost in Orange County — Honest Numbers

The first question every OC homeowner asks us is "how much does pool demolition cost?" Here are real numbers, not vague ranges:

Partial Pool Demolition (Fill-In / Abandonment)

The top 18–24 inches of the pool shell are broken out, drainage holes are added to the remaining structure, and the void is backfilled with compacted soil and gravel. This is the most affordable method.

Typical Cost: $8,000 – $15,000 depending on pool size and access. Permits still required.

Full Pool Removal (Complete Extraction)

The entire pool shell — gunite, plumbing, and all — is broken apart and hauled away. The excavation is backfilled, compacted in lifts, and graded. Leaves a fully usable yard with no restrictions on future building.

Typical Cost: $15,000 – $30,000 depending on pool size, depth, rebar density, and site access.

Both methods require a permit from your city's building department. C&S Demolition pulls all permits on your behalf and coordinates utility disconnections.

Why Orange County Homeowners Remove Their Pools

  • Backyard Remodel — Reclaim outdoor living space for a patio, garden, lawn, or outdoor kitchen.
  • Liability Reduction — Pools carry significant insurance liability. Removing an unused pool lowers your premium and eliminates drowning risk.
  • HOA Non-Compliance — Many older OC pools fall out of HOA safety standards. Removal resolves code issues permanently.
  • Selling the Home — In many OC neighborhoods, buyers prefer no pool. Removal can increase your buyer pool and sale price.
  • Ongoing Maintenance Costs — Pool maintenance in OC averages $200–$400/month. Removal eliminates that cost permanently.
  • ADU Development — The pool footprint is often the best location for a new accessory dwelling unit. Removal is the first step.
  • Water Conservation — Southern California's drought conditions make pool ownership increasingly expensive and controversial.

Our Pool Removal Process — Step by Step

  1. Free On-Site Assessment — We inspect the pool, measure dimensions, assess access (side yard width, overhead clearance), and identify the plumbing and electrical connections.

  2. Permit Application — C&S submits the demolition permit to your city's building department. Permit approval typically takes 2–4 weeks depending on the city.

  3. Drain & Disconnect Utilities — Pool is drained, electrical disconnected by licensed electrician, and gas/water lines capped. All utility shutoffs are coordinated.

  4. Demolition — Partial fill-in: top 18–24 inches broken out, drainage holes added. Full removal: entire shell demolished, broken into rubble and loaded for haul-out.

  5. Backfill & Compaction — Excavation is backfilled with clean fill soil, compacted in lifts to engineered specifications. Proper compaction prevents future settling.

  6. Grading & Final Inspection — Surface is rough-graded to match surrounding yard. Final city inspection is scheduled and attended by C&S.

Pool Removal FAQs & OC Service Area

How long does pool removal take in Orange County?

Active demolition work typically takes 2–5 days depending on pool size and removal method. Permit approval from your city adds 2–4 weeks before work can begin. C&S Demolition expedites permits wherever possible.

Do I need a permit to remove a pool in Irvine or Anaheim?

Yes. All OC cities require a demolition permit for pool removal, whether partial or full. C&S Demolition handles all permit applications and city coordination on your behalf.

Will removing my pool increase my home value?

In most OC neighborhoods, yes — particularly if the pool is older, underused, or the buyers you're targeting prefer yard space. Full removal has a more positive impact on appraisals and buyer perception than partial fill-in.

What happens to the gunite and concrete after removal?

Broken concrete and gunite are loaded into our dump trucks and transported to certified recycling facilities. Concrete is 100% recyclable and is typically crushed and reused as road base or fill material.
