Attached Garages Change the Calculation

When a garage is attached — sharing a wall with the living space, with a common roof plane and integrated foundation — demolishing it is not a standalone operation. The structural connection to the main house must be evaluated before a single concrete block is cut. In Portola Springs, where Toll Brothers, KB Homes, Shea Homes, and William Lyon Homes all used integrated garage designs as standard, the framing and shear wall configuration varies by builder and plan. We assess the structural interface before mobilizing to determine how the separation needs to be handled to leave the main dwelling envelope weathertight and structurally sound.

This matters especially for ADU conversion projects, which are among the most common reasons Portola Springs homeowners consider garage demolition today. California’s ADU laws have made garage conversions a popular path to adding living space, but in Portola Springs, the process has a layer that most online ADU guides skip: the Portola Springs Community Association’s ARC has its own position on ADU projects that affect the exterior appearance of the home, and that position must be addressed before the City of Irvine ADU permit is secured.

HOA Position on Garage-to-ADU Conversions in Portola Springs: The Portola Springs Community Association CC&Rs govern exterior modifications in significant detail. A garage-to-ADU conversion changes the front elevation of the home — the garage door disappears, materials change, and the streetscape shifts. The ARC requires an architectural submittal that includes elevation drawings, material specifications, and color samples for any conversion that alters the exterior. California law limits HOAs from outright prohibiting ADUs, but HOAs retain authority over exterior aesthetics. ARC approval must come before the city building permit is issued.

Common Questions

Can the HOA block my ADU garage conversion?

California law (AB 670 and related statutes) prohibits HOAs from outright banning ADUs. However, the Portola Springs Community Association retains authority over exterior design and aesthetics. The ARC can require design modifications, specific materials, or color matching to the existing streetscape. It cannot say no to the ADU concept, but it can require changes to how it looks. This is why working with an architect who knows the Portola Springs ARC process is important before you commit to a design.

Does demo of an attached garage require a permit from the City of Irvine?

Yes. Any structural demolition in Irvine requires a demolition permit from City of Irvine Building & Safety. For an attached garage, the permit application must address how the structure connects to the main dwelling and how that interface will be closed. ARC approval from the Portola Springs Community Association is required before the city will issue the permit.

How long does garage demolition take once permits are approved?

The actual demolition of a standard attached two-car garage in Portola Springs typically takes 1–3 days depending on scope. The pre-demolition process — ARC approval, SCAQMD survey, City of Irvine permit, utility disconnects — takes 6–12 weeks. We work all pre-demolition tracks simultaneously to compress that timeline.
