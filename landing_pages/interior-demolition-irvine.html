What Interior Demo in an Irvine Production Home Actually Involves

Irvine's residential housing stock is unusually well-documented compared to most California cities. Most homes in Woodbridge, Turtle Rock, Northwood, and Westpark were built by a small number of large production builders — William Lyon, Taylor Morrison, Shea Homes, Irvine Pacific — with consistent structural systems and standardized framing layouts. That's useful when you're planning interior demolition, because the load-bearing wall locations and MEP rough-in positions follow patterns.

That said, 30 or 40 years of renovations layer complexity on top of original construction. A 1978 Woodbridge home that's been kitchen-remodeled twice and had a bathroom addition may have three generations of electrical, plumbing, and tile work to navigate. C&S assesses what's there before we start swinging, not after.

For homes built before 1978, lead paint is a real consideration in selective demolition — especially on walls being retained adjacent to walls being demolished. Disturbing lead-containing paint on a wall you're keeping requires a different approach than demo-only work. C&S identifies the applicable conditions at the site walk and scopes the work accordingly.

Interior Demo Scopes C&S Handles in Irvine

Kitchen Gut

Full kitchen demolition: cabinets, countertops, tile backsplash, flooring, soffit, and fixture removal. Utility service points located and confirmed off before demo. Plumbing rough-in exposed for plumber access. Site cleaned and debris removed before your GC arrives for new installation.

Bathroom Tearout

Tub, shower, vanity, tile, flooring, and drywall removed down to studs and subfloor. Water supply and drain locations exposed. Older Irvine homes with original tile-on-mortar beds add weight and complexity — C&S accounts for the extra material in the demo plan and debris disposal estimate.

Open-Plan Conversion

Wall removal for open kitchen/living/dining layouts. Load-bearing assessment required before any structural wall comes down. C&S works from architect or structural engineer drawings. Temporary shoring installed where required. We take what the drawings specify — not an inch more.

Full-Gut Interior Demo

Complete interior demolition to studs, joists, and subfloor — preserving structural and exterior elements per the demo plan. Used for major remodels or when a home's interior is being fully replaced. C&S works with your project architect's demo drawings from start to finish.

Popcorn Ceiling Removal

Pre-1979 Irvine homes commonly have popcorn ceilings with asbestos content. SCAQMD-compliant asbestos abatement is required if the material tests positive — not standard demo. C&S coordinates with a licensed abatement contractor for regulated material and handles non-asbestos popcorn ceiling removal as part of interior demo scope.

Flooring Demo

Tile, hardwood, carpet, vinyl, and underlayment removal. Pre-1979 homes may have asbestos-containing vinyl floor tile or adhesive. If survey results flag these materials, abatement precedes C&S's demo work. For confirmed non-asbestos flooring, C&S removes and disposes of all material and leaves subfloor clean for the new installation.

Does Irvine Interior Demo Require a Permit?

It depends on the scope. Cosmetic demolition — removing tile, flooring, cabinets, and drywall that's being replaced in kind — typically doesn't require a standalone demolition permit. Wall removal that involves structural changes requires a structural permit regardless of whether it's part of a larger remodel. The City of Irvine Building Division makes this determination based on the specific work scope. C&S confirms the permit requirement before scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions

My Irvine home was built in 1985 — do I need asbestos testing before interior demo?

Yes. SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires a certified asbestos and lead-containing materials survey for all structures before demolition — regardless of construction date. A 1985 Irvine home has the same survey requirement as a 1975 home. This applies to interior demo projects that meet the SCAQMD threshold for disturbed material. The survey must be completed and on file before the City of Irvine will issue a permit for the work.

How long does an interior demo take in an Irvine home?

A single room — kitchen or bathroom — typically runs 1–2 days including debris removal. A full-gut interior demo on a 2,000 sq ft home runs 3–5 days. Scope complexity, flooring type, and whether asbestos abatement is required affects the timeline. C&S provides a specific timeline estimate at the site walk, not a range pulled from a general formula.

Does my HOA need to know about interior demolition?

Interior demolition that doesn't require a permit and doesn't affect exterior appearance generally doesn't require HOA notification under most Irvine village CC&Rs. Work that requires a City permit usually requires HOA notification per the CC&Rs — but not necessarily ARC pre-approval for interior-only scope. Check your specific CC&Rs, or C&S can review the relevant provisions with you. Requirements vary by village.

Can C&S demo a kitchen while the family is still living in the home?

Yes — C&S regularly performs interior demo in occupied homes. Dust containment barriers separate the work zone from living areas. We schedule heavy work during daytime hours, stage debris removal to avoid disrupting household routines, and leave the site broom-clean each day. The disruption period is minimized because the physical demo typically runs only 1–3 days on most Irvine residential scopes.
