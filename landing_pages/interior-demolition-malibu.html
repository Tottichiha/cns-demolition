Interior Demo on Malibu Properties — What's Different

Interior demolition on a Malibu property carries weight that a standard inland remodel doesn't. The homes along PCH, in the canyons, and on the bluffs above the ocean tend to have layered histories — additions over original construction, materials that span multiple decades, and finishes that weren't installed cheaply. When selective gut work is required, the demolition phase has to be executed with precision: take out exactly what the plans call for, protect everything else.

Beyond the physical complexity, Malibu's location within the South Coast AQMD jurisdiction means SCAQMD Rule 1403 governs all work that disturbs building materials — regardless of the structure's age. An asbestos and lead survey is required before any demolition activity begins. That step cannot be skipped.

SCAQMD Rule 1403 — Required for All Malibu Interior Demolition:

Before any interior demolition work disturbs building materials — walls, ceilings, flooring, mechanical systems — an asbestos and lead-containing materials survey must be completed. This requirement applies to structures of all ages. C&S coordinates with licensed environmental inspectors. If regulated materials are found, abatement is completed first. Demolition proceeds only after clearance.

Interior Demo Scopes C&S Handles in Malibu

Full Interior Gut
Complete stripping of interior to structural shell. Walls, ceilings, flooring, cabinets, fixtures, mechanical, electrical, plumbing rough-in — everything removed down to studs and slab. Used when full renovation or redesign is planned from the ground up.
Kitchen Tearout
Cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, and plumbing rough-in removed. Soffit demo if layout is changing. Careful disconnection of gas, plumbing, and electrical before any demo. C&S coordinates sequence with your contractor so the kitchen remodel has a clean start.
Bathroom Demo
Tile, tub, shower pan, vanity, fixtures, and flooring removed. Wet area demo requires proper containment and drainage management — especially in oceanfront or hillside properties with sensitive drainage systems. Subsurface waterproofing layers addressed as needed.
Wall & Partition Removal
Selective non-structural partition removal to open floor plans. Temporary shoring installed before any structural or load-bearing walls are addressed. C&S does not remove load-bearing walls without engineer sign-off — the structural plan must be in hand before that work begins.
Flooring Removal
Hardwood, tile, stone, vinyl, and carpet removed. Older adhesive layers (mastic) assessed for asbestos before removal — a common source on properties with pre-1990s flooring installations. C&S evaluates mastic samples as part of the pre-demo survey protocol.
Fire-Rebuild Interior
Smoke-damaged, heat-damaged, or burned interior materials classified and removed under appropriate hazmat protocols. Fire debris in Malibu may require specialized disposal documentation. C&S works with environmental consultants to establish the correct removal and disposal pathway for fire-affected interiors.

Selective Demo vs. Full Gut — Knowing the Difference Matters

On a Malibu renovation, the distinction between selective and full gut isn't just about scope — it changes the permit pathway, the hazmat approach, and what gets protected on site.

Factor Selective Interior Demo Full Interior Gut
Permit Usually covered under remodel permit; no separate demo permit May require separate demolition permit depending on scope and LA County review
Hazmat Survey Required — disturbing any building material triggers Rule 1403 Required — full scope survey across all materials being removed
Structural Concern Only where partition walls or adjacent structural elements are near the demo zone Shoring plan required; engineer coordination on any load-bearing elements
Protection Needed High — finished surfaces and systems adjacent to demo zone must be protected Lower — most surfaces are being removed anyway; perimeter protection is the focus
Timeline 1–3 days depending on scope 2–5 days for a full residential gut after hazmat clearance

Working Around What Stays

On high-end Malibu properties, C&S treats the surfaces that are NOT being demolished as protected assets. Floor protection, door and opening barriers, and clean debris removal routes are established before any work begins. Dust from demo work reaching finished areas — or debris dragged through spaces that aren't part of the scope — is avoidable with proper planning. That planning happens before the crew arrives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does all interior demo in Malibu require asbestos testing?

Yes — SCAQMD Rule 1403 requires an asbestos and lead-containing materials survey before any demolition or renovation that disturbs building materials. This applies to structures of all ages. C&S coordinates the survey with licensed environmental inspectors before scheduling any interior work.

Can C&S do demo while I'm still living in the property?

Not during active asbestos abatement — occupants must vacate for that phase. For selective demo without hazmat involvement, work scheduling depends on the scope and location within the home. C&S can phase work to minimize disruption in occupied properties, but the specific situation is assessed during the site visit.

Who handles load-bearing wall removal?

Load-bearing wall removal requires a structural engineer's sign-off and temporary shoring before any demo begins. C&S does not proceed with structural element removal without an approved shoring plan and the appropriate permit in hand. Your architect or structural engineer provides the plan; C&S executes the demo work.

How does C&S handle debris removal in Malibu?

All debris is loaded and hauled to licensed C&D recycling facilities. For hazmat-classified materials (asbestos, lead debris), separate disposal under licensed hazmat carrier and manifest requirements applies. C&S provides disposal documentation on request. Dumpsters are not typically left on-site overnight in residential Malibu areas without property owner arrangement — debris removal is scheduled to avoid extended street placement.
